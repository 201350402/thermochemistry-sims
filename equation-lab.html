Meet the equation

You will use one equation for the whole lab: burning methane gas.

Starting equation

ΔH =EXOTHERMIC

ΔH is negative, so heat is released. Burning 1 mol of CH₄ releases 890.4 kJ.

On every screen

  1. Compare the starting and new equations. What changed?
  2. Decide: Multiply × 2, Divide ÷ 2 or Reverse ⇄. Click it.
  3. Watch the new ΔH appear.

Symbol key

ΔH
enthalpy change, in kJ
−
heat released (exothermic)
+
heat absorbed (endothermic)
2, ½
coefficients: the big numbers in front of formulas
(g) (l)
gas, liquid

Remember

  1. Change the coefficients → ΔH changes by the same factor.
  2. Reverse the equation → same number, opposite sign.
